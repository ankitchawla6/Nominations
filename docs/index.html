The Upgrade

What is changing?

A shift from ad-hoc student support to a structured nomination pathway.

Before

Cialfo Guide support may have been seen as a backup option for students who needed extra help.

Now

Your school can nominate students into a clear support track with defined next steps and progress visibility.

Outcome

More personalized student guidance, stronger application readiness, and less operational load on counselors.

The school counselor remains central. The Cialfo Guide adds structured specialist support for nominated students.

Student Profile

Who should you nominate?

Students who would benefit from a more guided path, especially at critical moments in the application journey.

SOP support

Visa readiness

Students needing support with documents, timelines, and process clarity.

After submission

Students needing help with offers, deposits, or pre-departure.

More structure needed

Students or families who would benefit from additional confidence and follow-up.

Process

How the nomination pathway works

1
Nominate

Counselor identifies students who need additional guidance.

2
Review

Cialfo Guide reviews the student's stage, goals, and support needs.

3
Support

Guide connects with the student and provides targeted guidance.

4
Update

Counselor receives relevant progress updates and next steps.

The process is designed to be simple for counselors: nominate, stay informed, and continue guiding the student as the trusted school-side advisor.

Ownership

Clear responsibilities

Designed to remove ambiguity and make support easier for your school team.

School Counselor

Identifies students for nomination, shares school-side context where needed, and remains the trusted advisor throughout the journey.

Cialfo Guide

Owns nominated student progression across application readiness, SOP review, visa guidance, and post-application next steps.

K12 Client Partner

Owns the school relationship and remains your main contact for partnership, platform, and school-level support.

Your counselor team leads the relationship with the student; Cialfo Guide adds specialist support; your Client Partner keeps the school partnership running smoothly.
